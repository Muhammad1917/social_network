## Users

A broad public audience looking to discover and connect with specialized, niche communities. Users primarily engage through content discovery and consuming updates from their interests.

## Product Purpose

A social networking platform centered on niche community discovery, enabling users to find, join, and engage with groups of shared interest within a larger public ecosystem.

## Design Principles

- **Discovery-Driven**: The interface should intuitively guide users toward new, relevant communities and content.
- **Community-Centric**: Design should emphasize group identity and shared interest over individual-only feeds.
- **Seamless Consumption**: Content consumption should be fluid, low-friction, and highly engaging.
